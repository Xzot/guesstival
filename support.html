How to play

Act 12+ players

Forehead

Hold the phone sideways against your forehead with the screen facing the room. The round starts by itself once the phone is in place. Friends give clues without saying the word. Tilt the phone down when you guess it and up to pass. A round lasts 30, 60 or 90 seconds.

Act 22 to 6 teams

Explain It

The phone stays in the explainer's hands. Describe the word to your own team, swipe right when they get it and left to skip. Skips can cost a point, and you can switch that off on the ticket before you start. Play to 20, 30 or 50. Every team gets the same number of turns, so a game never ends halfway through a round.

Act 3New in 1.2

Who Am I?

Players take turns in the hot seat with the phone on their forehead. The room can see your secret identity and you can't. Ask yes-or-no questions, tilt down when you've got it, or tilt up to give up. If nobody knows the card, the room can swap it in the first 10 seconds. Most identities found wins, and a tie goes to whoever was faster.

Act 4No talking

Act It Out

Works like Forehead, but the clue-givers stay silent and act the word out. Only packs that can be mimed show up for this game, such as Animals, Actions, Jobs and the holiday packs.

Questions

The tilt doesn't react, or words skip on their own

Open the Sound desk from the bottom of the lineup poster and tap Check next to Level check. Hold the phone the way you play while it measures. If words still skip by themselves, lower Tilt sensitivity. You can also set Answer by to Tapping, and two big buttons take over from the tilt.

I bought packs, but they look locked

Tap Restore Purchases. It's at the bottom of the wristband wall and on the Sound desk. Make sure this iPhone is signed in to the same Apple Account you paid with. Packs bought by someone in your Family Sharing group should unlock the same way.

Why are the words in English?

The buttons and menus follow your iPhone's language, but every pack is written in English. If you'd rather play in your own language, type your words into the House Band.

How do I add players?

The first time you open the app, the gate asks who's here. Later, tap Who's here? or Edit the crowd on the lineup poster. You can add up to 12 names. The game uses them to call out whose turn it is, and with four or more it can deal teams for Explain It.

How do I add my own words?

Open House Band at the bottom of the lineup poster and type a word or phrase (30 characters at most). It holds up to 200 of them, it's free, and you can turn it on like any other pack in every game.

Film the crowd won't turn on

Filming needs the camera and the microphone. If you said no when iOS asked, open the iPhone's Settings › Guesstival and switch both back on, then try the toggle on the ticket again. Filming is there in Forehead and Act It Out.

Do I need the internet?

Only to see prices, buy a pack or restore purchases. The games themselves run offline, so a cabin or a plane is fine.
