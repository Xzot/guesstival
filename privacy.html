Data not collected

The short versionAdmit one

Guesstival has no account to sign up for and no server behind it. It shows no ads, runs no analytics, doesn't track you and contains no code from other companies. The App Store privacy label says Data Not Collected, and that is the whole story.

Who's behind it

I'm Vladyslav Shchuka, an independent developer, and I make Guesstival on my own. "I" and "me" on this page means me. This policy covers the Guesstival app for iPhone.

Kept on your iPhone

The app saves a few things in its own storage on the phone, so the next party starts where the last one ended:

  • your settings: sound, vibration, tilt sensitivity, the level check, how you answer, round length, team names, the target score, which packs were switched on and whether Family words is set;
  • a note of which packs you own, so the wristband wall opens without waiting for the App Store;
  • the words you've already played, so they don't come back too soon;
  • the player names typed at the gate (12 at most);
  • the words you write into the House Band (200 at most).

None of this is uploaded or sent to me. If you delete the app, iOS deletes all of it too. Your iPhone's own backup may include it, the same way it includes any app's settings.

Motion sensor

In the games where the phone sits on someone's forehead, Guesstival reads the motion sensor to notice a tilt. Each reading is used on the spot to count an answer and then thrown away.

Film the crowd

This option is off until you switch it on, on the ticket for Forehead or Act It Out. iOS asks for camera and microphone access the first time. While it's on, the front camera and the microphone record only during the round you're playing, so you can watch your friends' faces afterwards.

The clip is put together on the phone (that's where the captions get added) and sits in a temporary folder inside the app. It's deleted when the next round starts or when you close the game. The app never uploads it, and I never see it.

A clip leaves your iPhone only if you send it somewhere yourself through the iOS share sheet. From then on, the app or person you shared it with handles it. If you pick Save Video, iOS asks for permission to add to your photo library. That permission lets Guesstival save a video and nothing more; it can't look at your photos. You can turn camera, microphone and Photos access off any time in the iPhone's Settings › Guesstival.

Buying packs

Apple sells the packs through the App Store. I don't get your card details, your name or your email. The app only asks the App Store which packs your Apple Account owns, so it can unlock them.

Going online

The games play offline. The app connects to the App Store to load prices, make a purchase or restore one, and that's it.

Kids and families

Guesstival is rated 4+ and made for family evenings as much as for adults. Because it collects nothing from anyone, it collects nothing from children either. Names you type in for younger players stay on the phone with everything else.

If this changes

I'll update this page and the date at the top. A change that starts collecting data would also change the App Store label, so you'd see it there before you update.
