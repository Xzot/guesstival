A word game you play with the people around you. Somebody holds the phone, everybody else shouts, mimes or explains. Four games, one iPhone, and it plays without a connection.

Tonight's lineup

  • Forehead The phone sits on your forehead and your friends yell clues. Tilt down when you know it, tilt up to pass.
  • Explain It Two to six teams take turns describing words to each other and race to a target score.
  • Who Am I? Everyone gets a secret identity in turn. Ask yes-or-no questions until you work out yours.
  • Act It Out Forehead with the sound off. Your friends can only mime.

Bring your own crowd

Type in who showed up (up to 12 names) and the game passes the phone around for you, keeps tonight's scores and can split everyone into fair teams. Got inside jokes? Write them into the House Band, a deck of your own words that works in every game.
